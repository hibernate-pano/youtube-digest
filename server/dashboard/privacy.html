Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-14

What this service is

YouTube Digest is an open-source Chrome extension (bring-your-own-key) with an optional GitHub-account sync service. This page covers the sync service at ytd.panbo.space.

What we store

  • Your GitHub identity (id, login name) so your account can be recognised.
  • Your notes, vocabulary and review progress that you save while signed in.

Data is stored in a Neon (PostgreSQL) database and is isolated per GitHub account: the server only ever serves the rows belonging to the authenticated user.

What we never do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • Transcript and AI requests go directly from the extension to Supadata and your chosen AI provider under their own terms; the sync service is not involved.

Your controls

  • Export: download all your data as JSON from the dashboard (Export button).
  • Delete: delete your account and all your data from the dashboard (Delete account button). This is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Sign out: stops the extension from syncing; your data remains in your account.

Security

Sign-in uses GitHub OAuth; the extension stores only a short-lived token. Connections are HTTPS. The database is not publicly accessible.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project repository (github.com/hibernate-pano/youtube-digest).
